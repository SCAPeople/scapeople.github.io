The problem

You raised the round. Now you have to rapidly grow your headcount.

Hiring is the last thing a fast-growing company builds on purpose, and the first thing that breaks.

The pressure is real

Funding closes and the plan calls for two to four times the team inside a year. The hiring happens whether anyone is ready or not.

The job looks covered

A founder picked it up. Or a Chief of Staff is running a management consulting playbook that was never built for a company at this stage.

So it gets built wrong

Job titles invented one offer at a time. Pay set by whoever negotiated hardest. The bad hires surface later.

The fix is hard to judge

The answer is a Head of People. But you have never done that job, so you cannot tell a strong one from a confident one.
